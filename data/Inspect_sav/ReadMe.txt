METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION

1. Description of methods used for collection/generation of data: 

This dataset contains the results of a online survey, which examined Ontario residents’ attitudes towards school board trustees. In addition, other questions within the survey focused on attitudes towards politics and current events, as well as demographic related questions.

The online survey was conducted by Dynata between November 21st and December 2nd, 2020. A total of 2,541 people over the age of 18 participated in the survey, with an oversampling of parents with school aged children (1,318 of the total respondents).

Sampling:  A total of 2,541 people over the age of 18 participated in the survey, with an oversampling of parents with school aged children (1,318 of the total respondents). Results of this survey have been weighted by parental status, age, gender, and region of Ontario.
